## Hotspot Interpretation

- Humanizer kept `ByteSize` at rank 1. `DefaultFormatter` moved from 8 to 7,
  while `OrdinalDatePattern` moved from 7 to 8 despite COG 51; coupling findings
  remain the primary ranking signal and complexity remains a bounded assist.
- `SuffixScaleWordsToNumberConverter` entered Humanizer's Top 10 with CYC 25 /
  COG 33. The change is explained by Sonar nesting and logical-sequence rules.
- FluentValidation's Top 10 ordering was unchanged. `LanguageManager` reports
  CYC 63 / COG 12, including null-flow operators in Cyclomatic only.
- MediatR's Top 10 ordering was unchanged. `ServiceRegistrar` reports CYC 14 /
  COG 28 after applying nesting and logical-sequence rules.
- Self semantic keeps `CSharpDependencyAnalyzer` at rank 1 with CYC 18 / COG 19.
  The pseudo-cycle fix removes noise without hiding the existing analysis
  responsibility hotspot.

## Decision

removed, external analysis contracts are stable, complexity deltas are
